# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Analysis (2014–2017)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance from 2014 to 2017 using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify sales trends, regional performance, customer segment contributions, profitability patterns, and delivery efficiency through an interactive dashboard.

The project leverages Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Dashboard Design techniques to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

---

## ⭐ Project Highlights

- Analyzed retail sales data from 2014–2017.
- Built an interactive Excel dashboard.
- Evaluated sales, profit, and growth performance.
- Identified top-performing regions and customer segments.
- Analyzed discount impact on profitability.
- Generated business recommendations based on insights.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Total sales reached **$2.29 Million**.
- Total profit exceeded **$286K**.
- The **West Region** generated the highest sales.
- The **Technology Category** delivered the highest profitability.
- The **Consumer Segment** contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Higher discounts negatively impacted profitability.
- Sales consistently peaked during November and December.
- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping method.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory before peak seasonal demand periods.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing regions.
- Reduce excessive discounting to improve profit margins.
- Expand profitable technology product offerings.
- Improve delivery efficiency through optimized shipping strategies.
